## Platform

web

## Users

Broad/mixed — anyone who needs help with AI + data work: small businesses, other companies, or individual clients. No single narrow vertical; copy should speak to "someone who needs AI+data help," not be written as if only addressing SMBs or only enterprises.

## Product Purpose

The site is turning from a portfolio/demo into a **services site**: Uri Iron (BI analyst & automation developer) offers to build AI-powered data solutions for paying clients, and the site's job is to get a visitor to understand what he can build for them and reach out to hire him — not just to admire past work. Existing interactive dashboard demos remain, but move to a secondary "Projects" page shown respectfully with credit per project, rather than being the homepage's centerpiece.

## Positioning

Leads with **Power BI dashboards and data/workflow automation** (HTML tools + Power BI) — his strongest, most evidenced area: he builds operational Power BI dashboards used for real daily decisions in his day job, and automations that save real manual hours. AI's honest role in the pitch is **process, not product-feature**: he builds with AI-assisted tools (Claude Code, MCP-connected Power BI development) to work faster and better — the site must NOT claim the delivered dashboards themselves contain embedded AI features for the end viewer (no evidence of that yet). The differentiator: someone who has actually shipped operational BI tools inside a real organization (recruiting, absence, enrollment dashboards in daily use) and builds fast using modern AI-assisted tooling — not a generalist AI consultant without BI depth.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Current published service lineup (as of 2026-07-26), confirmed by Uri — do not expand without him saying so:** (1) Power BI dashboards — lead service, (2) data/workflow automation (HTML tools + Power BI). **Chatbots are explicitly NOT a published service right now** — Uri wants to go deeper on chatbot skills first and will say when it's ready to publish. Do not show a chatbot service card, tag, or claim anywhere on the live site until he confirms otherwise. (The Ariel HR chatbot project and its RAG pipeline still exist as real, private evidence of capability — just not a public-facing service offering yet.)
- **AI framing:** always a process/tooling claim ("I build with AI-assisted tools so it's faster and better"), never a claim that AI is a feature inside the delivered product itself, unless Uri confirms that's true for a specific engagement.
- **Certifications section (new, pending real assets):** a "Certifications" section is wanted. Uri will supply real certificate details for Claude to add as they're ready — do not fabricate certificate names, issuers, or dates. Build the section to be easy to extend.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with Power BI dashboards and data/workflow automation — the two services actually published right now. Chatbots stay off the public site until Uri says he's ready.
2. Never expose real organizational data from the university employer — dashboards stay 100% synthetic, same constraint as today, just repositioned as case-study proof instead of the homepage centerpiece.
3. Stay honest about what's actually provable today: AI is a process claim (helps Uri build faster/better), never a claim of AI features embedded in delivered dashboards. Named real-project evidence is currently limited to רגש plus the anonymized university work. Don't write copy that implies more of a track record than exists.
4. Certifications are a real, evolving section — ship with what's provided (1 cert now, 2 more incoming) and design it to be trivially extendable, not a fixed fabricated set.
5. Broad audience — don't narrow copy to one vertical or company size.
