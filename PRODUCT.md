# Product

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers for BI / data analyst roles in Israel (central region). They open the link from a CV or LinkedIn, often on a phone, and decide within seconds whether to read further.

## Product Purpose (since 2026-11-18)

A job-search portfolio. The site's one job: make a recruiter want to interview Uri Iron for a BI / data analyst role, and make it trivial to download the CV or reach him. It replaced the July 2026 "AI + data services" positioning; there is no services page and no client-facing pitch anymore.

## What the home page carries, in order

1. Hero: the automation story ("a report that took 4 hours a week now updates itself") with the particle cloud that turns into a bar chart, plus CTAs: dashboards, CV PDF, LinkedIn.
2. Four facts: 5 dashboards in daily use, 60+ DAX measures in one, 4 hours/week automated, under-10-seconds answer target.
3. Three dashboard recreations (of five), with the synthetic-data note.
4. Experience and education timeline.
5. Contact: email, LinkedIn, phone, CV.

## Rules

- Every fact must come from `job-search-assistant/baseline-resume/baseline.md`. Never invent experience, numbers or certificates.
- Dashboards stay 100% synthetic. Never publish or reference the employer's real data.
- The military-service entry is not used (Uri, 2026-11-12).
- AI is a process claim (he builds with Claude Code / MCP), never a feature claim about delivered dashboards.
- No emoji as icons anywhere.
